Small status/role/activity-type pill used in table rows, cards, and list screens.

```jsx
<Badge status="success">Confirmed</Badge>
<Badge status="warning">Awaiting reply</Badge>
<Badge kind="activity" activity="hiking">Hiking</Badge>
```

Activity chips use a neutral-tinted background with a small colored dot rather than colored text, to keep contrast safe across all 5 activity hues.
