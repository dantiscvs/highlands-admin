Bookings

Forward the confirmation. We read it.

Every trip gets its own email address. The flight, train or hotel comes back as a proposal you accept — nothing is written behind your back.

  • Forward, paste or upload — email, plain text or PDF.
  • Everything goes through review, so a bad read cannot corrupt the plan.
  • Locked fields can never be overwritten by an import.

The group

Not everyone carries everything

  • Assign to everyone or to specific people, per item.
  • Everyone ticks their own — and you can see who has not.
  • Same model for tasks, from insurance to bike service.

Not only for cyclists

Pick the trip type and the app adapts — which fields appear, how they are labelled, and how pace is estimated.

🚴

Cycling

Distance, ascent and surface, with speed per surface type and a climbing rate.

🥾

Hiking & trekking

Terrain instead of surface, and climbing time the way Naismith's rule does it.

🚐

Driving & campervan

No ascent column. Costs become the headline metric instead of climbing.

🛶

Kayaking & paddling

Water conditions replace surface, with paddling speed set per condition.

🚆

Public transport

Distance optional, pace off, logistics front and centre.

🧭

Mixed

Segment types for trips that ride, walk and paddle in one week.
